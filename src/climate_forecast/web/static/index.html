ClimateSpatiotemporalNet Architecture

The model combines multi-scale spherical harmonic Fourier positional encodings with Dilated Residual Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCN) and Multi-Head Temporal Self-Attention to predict multi-horizon probabilistic quantiles (P10, P50, P90) and extreme climate hazard warnings (Heatwaves, Frosts, Deluges) from historical daily observations ($T_{max}, T_{min}, PRCP$).

Hugging Face Hub Pipeline Tag: time-series-forecasting

Forecast Horizons: 1 to 14 Days Direct Multi-Step Rollout
